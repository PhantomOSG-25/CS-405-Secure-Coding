# Encryption Activity

## Overview

The encryption work in CS-405 reinforced the idea that sensitive information needs protection at more than one point in its life cycle. I completed the Module Five encryption coding activity successfully and carried the same concepts into the Green Pace security policy.

## Security Policy Connection

For Project One and Project Two, I separated data protection into three states:

- **Data at rest**: information stored in databases, computers, servers, or backups.
- **Data in flight**: information moving between systems, where secure connections such as HTTPS are used.
- **Data in use**: information actively being processed, where access should be limited to approved users and systems.

## What I Learned

Encryption is not one switch that protects information everywhere. The protection has to match what is happening to the data at that moment.

I relate it to handling customer information during a workday. Information still needs protection whether it is stored, being carried or transmitted, or open and actively being used. Its location changes, but the need to protect it does not.
